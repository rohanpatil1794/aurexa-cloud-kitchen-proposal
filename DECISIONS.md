## Stack, build and deploy

- Pinned to React 18.3 / @react-three/fiber 8 / drei 9 / three 0.170 (the stack the brief names) with Vite 7, TypeScript 5.9 and Tailwind CSS 4 (CSS-first config).
- Wall geometry module is src/scene/building/wallModel.ts (NOT walls.ts): on Windows/macOS walls.ts and Walls.tsx collide (extensionless './Walls' resolves to walls.ts first, and tsc raises TS1261 casing errors), so the pure model, helpers (wallBoxesIntersectSegment, wallCrossings, WALL_MODEL) live in wallModel.ts.
- Build: index.html is a template. Its {{SITE_TITLE}} / {{SITE_DESCRIPTION}} / {{STUDIO_NAME}} / {{OG_DESCRIPTION}} / {{OG_IMAGE}} / {{OG_IMAGE_ALT}} tokens are filled from src/config.ts by the html-meta plugin in vite.config.ts (dev and build), so the tab title and link previews follow the config; og:image / twitter:image are made absolute (crawlers do not resolve a relative URL; it used to be a hand-edited /og.png) from SITE_URL or Vercel's VERCEL_PROJECT_PRODUCTION_URL, and when neither is set they stay /og.png and the build prints a warning. public/og.png carries KITCHEN_NAME as a picture, so run `npm run assets` after changing it.
- Build: fingerprinted JS / CSS / fonts are emitted to dist/static (build.assetsDir) instead of dist/assets, because public/assets/floorplan.png is not fingerprinted and lives in /assets; this lets vercel.json cache /static as immutable and keep /assets revalidated.
- Build: the Stage is loaded with React.lazy (scene/LazyStage.tsx), so the first paint needs only the vendor (react) and index chunks (about 140 KB gzipped); three, r3f, drei, camera-controls and the scene (about 320 KB gzipped) load afterwards in parallel. A chunk that fails to load shows the WebGL fallback (floor plan) and releases the splash instead of blanking the page. manualChunks is a function that pins only react / react-dom / scheduler (vendor) and three; everything else follows its importers.
- Build: Public Sans is declared with woff2-only @font-face rules in src/index.css (the six .woff duplicates that @fontsource/*.css adds are gone from dist); the 400 / 500 / 600 files the hero uses are preloaded by the preload-fonts plugin. textTexture.ts also waits for the 800 weight (EXIT / GAS / name-plate signs).
- Build: shared links work: /#safety (any nav id) scrolls to that section flush under the top bar and releases the splash (the Canvas does not draw while the stage is off screen); /#stage opens the explorer once the scene is ready (lib/deepLink.ts).
- Deploy: vercel.json sets framework vite, immutable caching for /static, revalidation for html and the un-hashed public files, X-Robots-Tag noindex plus a CSP (self only, inline styles allowed, blob / data images and workers), X-Frame-Options SAMEORIGIN, nosniff, a strict Referrer-Policy and a closed Permissions-Policy. camera-controls is declared in package.json (CameraRig imports it directly).

## Stage, camera and lighting

- Stage: the explorer panel offset uses camera.setViewOffset (image slides left by half of 360 px on desktop; up by 7% / 20% of the height for the mobile sheet's peek / half states, a guess at the sheet heights), not a camera move, so perspective and picking stay exact; dollyToCursor is off because a view offset would skew cursor-anchored zoom.
- Stage: orbit poses are viewport-aware (data/cameras.ts fitOrbitPose): narrow or panel-reduced viewports dolly out up to 3.4x so the footprint keeps fitting the width; portrait phones also swing the aerial / hero view towards frontal and higher. Eye level widens its lens on portrait screens (lookFov). Preset numbers for aerial, plan and hero were retuned for the 1440 x 900 + 360 px panel case; targets sit on the plinth's centre column so the turntable never swings the model.
- Stage: the mouse wheel is left to the page during the hero phase (camera-controls would preventDefault it and trap the page scroll); wheel dolly starts when the explorer is entered.
- Stage: the plinth is one merged outline (footprint + 1.5 ft margin, plus a rounded notch for the pickup apron) at a single height, so PickupZone can draw on y = 0.02 like everything else. Floors are one merged mesh per material (7 draw calls) with world-aligned procedural textures; the zone tint is mixed in sRGB (55% towards the zone colour); threshold strips are 3 x 0.5 ft just inside each zone-room door and fade with the zones layer.
- Stage: environment panels are plain meshes with Lightformer's material (so their colours can be damped between Day and Evening and the cube re-rendered only while easing); the key light is a south-west DirectionalLight whose shadow camera is re-fitted to the model every time the light moves.
- Stage: `?quality=low|high` in the URL pins the quality tier and disables the PerformanceMonitor (diagnostics); 'low' = 1024 shadow map refreshed every other frame, dpr 1.

## Labels, picking and selection

- Labels: one DOM layer driven by a single useFrame (not one drei <Html> per room). The layer is appended beside R3F's event element (drei's Html lives inside it, so pill pointer events bubble into the scene's picking), and one loop can project, fade, resolve overlaps and z-order all 23 pills. `pillPointer.over` (labelsLayout.ts) stops R3F's late "pointer left the canvas" from clearing a hover a pill has just set.
- Labels: pills that would overlap are stacked / shifted on screen (Declutter, smallest rooms keep their spot) and tied to their room's floor centre by a thin stem when moved more than 14 px; at 390 px wide this keeps all 23 labels without collisions. The clamp keeps pills clear of the top bar and (desktop) the explorer panel; the mobile bottom-sheet cover mirrors twice CameraRig's SHEET_LIFT.
- Labels: orbit views show every label and fade them only for near-horizontal views (camera below ~24 degrees above the label); eye level keeps labels within 12-32 ft; in full-height mode labels hide while the camera is under the ceiling. Other labels dim while a room is selected (less the further they are from it); the selected pill is orange, hovered pills reveal the area tag.
- Picking: the cursor is set inline on the canvas (the stage CSS gives the canvas its own `grab` cursor, which `document.body.style.cursor` would not override). Hover is ignored while a mouse button is down (orbiting); on touch the tint shows only while the finger is down.
- Selection loop is drawn on the floor (just inside the wall faces) and on the wall tops (above the ceiling slab in full height), with its on-screen width kept near 5.5 px whatever the camera distance.

## Safety and dimensions

- Safety points are generated, not typed (src/data/safety.ts): wall-hung items take the nearest free wall spot (solid wall, clear of every door's 3 ft landing, glazing and floor-standing equipment, so they move on their own when equipment changes); a room fitted wall to wall hangs its extinguisher in the corridor beside the door; spots on walls the SW aerial cannot see cost 2.5 ft of extra distance. SafetyPoint gained an optional rot (facing, EquipItem.rot convention).
- Safety: smoke detectors are in every room (two in the Main Hot Kitchen) as briefed, with heat detectors added in the kitchen (2 x 2) and bakery; LPG detectors hang low on the corridor walls beside the gas ring main (it runs through the corridors round the kitchen) plus one inside the kitchen; the kitchen has 4 extinguishers (one per door); cold storage, toilets, lift and garden have none of their own (stair: one in the corridor beside its door).
- Safety markers are drawn 1.2 to 1.35 x life size (icons in space), ceiling items stay at ceiling height even in dollhouse mode, and the pulse rings (one instanced shader, phase from position, fade out as the camera comes within ~10 ft) are static under reduced motion. Exit signs and the gas tag are double-sided.
- Dimensions: the 60 ft line runs along the SOUTH edge, 3.5 ft beyond the pickup apron (the north edge sits under the top bar in the plan preset; south frames the near corner of the aerial and stays in plan), the 50 ft line along the west edge; the north arrow sits just east of the plinth near the NE corner and glides to a smaller, further-south spot as the camera comes overhead (in plan the top bar covers everything north of the footprint and the panel leaves a ~6 ft strip).
